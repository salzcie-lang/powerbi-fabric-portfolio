Flow 04 · Email attachment capture

Systems that can only email still feed the platform

A building-management system sends its daily trend export as an attachment. The flow saves it to the landing zone, where the same alert and dataflows pick it up.
FILE INTAKE AUTOMATION  ·  07 / 08

01Filtered at the trigger

A subject filter and an attachment requirement sit on the trigger itself, so the rest of the mailbox never starts a run.

02Only data attachments are saved

Inline images and signatures are filtered out; only .csv and .xlsx attachments reach the folder.

03Large files are handled

The copy uses chunked transfer, so multi-megabyte exports do not fail on size.
